How to Play

🎮 Basics

Drag the three shapes from the tray onto the 8x8 grid. Fill a complete row or column to clear it. Clearing several lines at once, or on consecutive moves, builds a combo multiplier. The game ends when none of your shapes fit on the grid.

☣ Virus Protocol

Every few moves a virus node lands on the grid. The number on it shows how many moves remain before it copies itself into a neighboring empty cell. Purge a virus by clearing the row or column it sits in, or by hitting it with a bomb. Each purge earns bonus points.

🛡️ Firewall

Every virus you purge charges your Firewall. When all three bars are full you get a free bomb (you can hold up to 5).

🎲 Reroll and 💣 Bomb

Reroll replaces the three shapes in your tray. Bomb clears a 3x3 area: tap the bomb button, then tap a cell. When you run out, the button changes to "Watch +1", which lets you watch an optional short video ad for one more use.

Leaderboard

🏆 How does it work?

Your best score is synced automatically to the global leaderboard. You don't need an account.

🎲 How do I change my player name?

Open the Leaderboard and tap "New Name". Names are generated from a fixed list of words, so the leaderboard never shows offensive text.

🗑️ How do I delete my leaderboard entry?

Email us the player name shown on your Leaderboard screen and we will remove it within 30 days.

Other Questions

🔊 How do I mute the sound?

Tap the speaker icon at the top of the screen.

📡 Can I play offline?

Yes, the puzzle works without an internet connection. The leaderboard and ads need a connection.
