Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

🎮 How does the scoring system work?

You score 50 points for every placed block. Clearing full horizontal or vertical lines awards massive bonuses (500 pts for 1 line, up to 10,000+ pts for multiple lines). Consecutive line clears trigger Combo Multipliers (x2, x3, x4...)!

🏆 How does the Global Leaderboard work?

Your highest score is automatically synchronized to the Firebase Cloud Leaderboard. You can customize your nickname at any time by tapping the ✏️ edit icon on the Leaderboard screen.

💣 What are the Power-ups?

Reroll (🎲): Refreshes the three shapes in your tray.
Bomb (💣): Tap the bomb icon, then tap any tile on the board to blast a 3x3 area!

🔊 How can I mute sounds?

You can toggle audio effects on or off by tapping the 🔊 speaker icon in the top header.
