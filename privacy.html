Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This policy explains what data Cyber Grid: Neon Puzzle ("the game", "we", "us") processes, why, and the choices you have. The game is developed by Hüseyin Berat Yılmaz, an independent developer.

1. No Account Required

You can play without creating an account. We never ask for your real name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location.

Your player name and best score are visible to other players on the leaderboard. Please don't use your real name or other personal information as your player name. We do not use any analytics SDK, we do not sell your data, and the game has no in-app purchases.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded video ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information, the advertising identifier (IDFA), IP address (used to estimate coarse location), and ad interaction and diagnostic data.

  • App Tracking Transparency (iOS): The IDFA is only used for personalized ads if you allow tracking in the system prompt. If you decline, AdMob serves non-personalized ads. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: A consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform asks for your choices before any ads are requested.
  • Rewarded videos are always optional. They only play when you tap a button labeled "Watch +1".

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboard is active. To delete your entry, email us with the player name shown on the Leaderboard screen and we will remove it within 30 days. Uninstalling the game removes all data stored on your device.

6. Children's Privacy

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We will update this page if the way the game handles data changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
