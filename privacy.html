Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Welcome to Neon Block Blast: Puzzle ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe, transparent gaming experience.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require users to create an account or provide sensitive personal information (such as real name, email address, physical address, or phone number) to play the game.

  • Gameplay & Leaderboard Data: We store your chosen nickname and high scores anonymously using Firebase Firestore to provide the global leaderboard.
  • Device Identifiers & Analytics: We use Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymized gameplay metrics (such as levels completed, combos, and crash logs) to optimize game performance.
  • Advertising Data: We use Google AdMob to serve in-game advertisements. AdMob may collect and use device advertising identifiers (IDFA) in accordance with Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework to provide personalized or contextual ads.

2. Third-Party Services

Our game integrates trusted third-party SDKs that may process data under their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob & Privacy
  • Apple App Store Privacy

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Neon Block Blast: Puzzle does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Scores and nicknames are stored on secure Cloud Firestore servers. Users can reset their nickname or request score deletion at any time by contacting our support team.
