CYBERGRID

A neon block puzzle under attack. Fill lines, stop the viruses before they spread, and climb the global leaderboard.

☣ Virus Protocol

Virus nodes land on the grid and copy themselves when their countdown runs out. Clear their row or column before they take over.

🛡️ Firewall

Purge viruses with line clears to charge your Firewall and earn a free 3x3 bomb. The longer you last, the higher the Threat Level.

💥 Combos

Clear several lines at once, or on back-to-back moves, to multiply your score.

🎲 Reroll & 💣 Bomb

Swap out a tough tray or blast a 3x3 area when you're stuck.

🏆 Global Leaderboard

Pick your own player name and compete worldwide. No account needed.

📡 Play Offline

The puzzle works anywhere, no connection required. Available in English and Turkish.
